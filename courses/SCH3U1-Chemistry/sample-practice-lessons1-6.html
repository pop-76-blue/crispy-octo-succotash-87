15. In your own words, explain why atomic radius decreases across a period. (1 — self-check)
Nuclear charge increases while shielding stays the same, so the nucleus pulls the valence shell in more tightly, shrinking the radius.

24. Why does a lone pair compress bond angles more than a bonding pair does? (1 — self-check)
A lone pair is held by only one nucleus (the central atom's), so it spreads out more and takes up more space than a bonding pair, which is pulled between two nuclei.
