Polyatomic Ions

Unit 2: Chemical Nomenclature · Multi-atom ions that act as a single unit, and the -ate/-ite/per-/hypo- family pattern.

A polyatomic ion's charge comes from the group as a whole — name a compound containing one just like any other ionic compound (metal/cation name + polyatomic ion name), and don't change the polyatomic ion's name or charge.

The -ate / -ite / per- / hypo- family

Some elements (especially chlorine) form a whole family of oxyanions with the same charge but different numbers of oxygen atoms. The "-ate" form is the reference point you memorize; the others are named relative to it:

The charge on the ion stays the same across the whole family — only the number of oxygens (and the prefix/suffix) changes.
