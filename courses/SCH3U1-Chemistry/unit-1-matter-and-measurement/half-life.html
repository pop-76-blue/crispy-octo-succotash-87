Half-Life

Unit 1, Lesson 3 · How long it takes for half a radioactive sample to decay.

Half-life (t½): the time it takes for half of a radioactive sample to decay. It's a constant for a given isotope — it doesn't matter how much you start with, half of whatever remains disappears every half-life.

Finding an exact time (non-whole half-lives)

If the fraction remaining doesn't land on a clean half-life (like 20% instead of 25% or 50%), use:

Nt = N₀ (½)n   where n = t / t½

Solve for n using logarithms: n = log(Nt/N₀) ÷ log(0.5), then multiply n by the half-life to get the elapsed time.
