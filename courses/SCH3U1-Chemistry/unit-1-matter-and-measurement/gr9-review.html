Classifying matter

Click through the questions below to see how any sample of matter gets sorted.

Is the composition of the sample uniform throughout?
Yes → Mixture
No → Pure Substance
Element — a pure substance that can't be broken down chemically (e.g. O₂, Fe).
Compound — a pure substance made of two+ elements chemically combined (e.g. H₂O).
Homogeneous mixture (solution) — uniform throughout (e.g. salt water).
Heterogeneous mixture — not uniform; includes colloids (particles stay suspended, e.g. milk) and suspensions (particles settle, e.g. sand in water).

Signs of a chemical change

Colour/odour/pH change  ·  a precipitate forms  ·  light or heat is produced or absorbed  ·  a gas is produced  ·  a genuinely new substance is produced (the real test — the others are just clues).
