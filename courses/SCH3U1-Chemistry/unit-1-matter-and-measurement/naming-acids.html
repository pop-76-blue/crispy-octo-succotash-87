Naming Acids

Unit 2: Chemical Nomenclature · Binary (hydro-) acids and oxyacids, written (aq).

A compound releasing H⁺ in aqueous solution is an acid. There are two families: binary acids (H + one other nonmetal) and oxyacids (H + a polyatomic ion containing oxygen).

Binary acids

Pattern: "hydro" + nonmetal stem + "-ic acid". Example: HCl (aq) → hydrochloric acid.
FormulaName
HF (aq)Hydrofluoric acid
HBr (aq)Hydrobromic acid
HI (aq)Hydroiodic acid
H₂S (aq)Hydrosulfuric acid

Oxyacids

An oxyacid is just H⁺ added to a polyatomic ion that already contains oxygen. The ion's suffix changes: -ate → -ic acid, -ite → -ous acid. The per-/hypo- prefixes carry straight over.
