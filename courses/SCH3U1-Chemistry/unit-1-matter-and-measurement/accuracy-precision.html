Accuracy & Precision

Unit 1: Measurement & Matter · How close, and how consistent.

Accuracy

How close a measurement is to the true or accepted value.

Precision

How close a set of measurements are to each other — reproducibility.

A set of measurements can be precise but not accurate (tightly clustered, but off-target), accurate but not precise (scattered, but centred on the target), both, or neither.
