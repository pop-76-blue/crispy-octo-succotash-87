Metric Prefixes

Unit 1: Measurement & Matter · Moving between kilo, the base unit, centi, and milli.

Quiz reminder: memorize the relationships between milli, centi, the base unit (no prefix), and kilo. Any other prefix (like nano or mega) will be given to you if needed.

The ladder

Each step is a power of ten. Moving right multiplies by 10; moving left divides by 10.

kilo
×1000
→
base
×1
→
centi
×0.01
→
milli
×0.001
Example: 1 km = 1000 m (base unit). 1 m = 100 cm. 1 m = 1000 mm. Notice centi and milli both compare to the base unit, not to each other directly.
