Quick self-test

If you can do all of these without looking anything up, you're in good shape:

1. How many sig figs in 0.04500?

2. Round 12.4 + 3.75 to the correct number of decimal places.

3. What's the name of element symbol "Mg"? What about "P"?

4. Convert 3.2 km to cm.

5. An element has isotopes at 62.94 u (69.2%) and 64.93 u (30.8%). What's its average atomic mass?

6. What's the difference between accuracy and precision, in your own words?
