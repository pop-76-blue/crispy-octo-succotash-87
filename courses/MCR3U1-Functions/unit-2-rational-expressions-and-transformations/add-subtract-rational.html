Adding and Subtracting Rational Expressions

MCR3U1 · Unit 2 · Rational expressions (adding and subtracting)

You add and subtract rational expressions exactly as you do fractions: you need a common denominator first. AB + CB = A + CB.

The method

  1. Factor every denominator.
  2. Find the LCD: every different factor, each used the highest number of times it appears.
  3. Rewrite each fraction over the LCD by multiplying its top and bottom by the missing factors.
  4. Combine the numerators. Put brackets around a numerator that is being subtracted, then distribute the minus sign.
  5. Factor and reduce if you can.
  6. State the restrictions from the original denominators (every factor).
