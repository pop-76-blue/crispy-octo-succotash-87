Before you start: the habits that earn marks

  • Factor first. Every rational expression question starts with factoring. Fast factoring is the whole game.
  • Cancel factors, not terms. In (x + 5)/x you can't cancel the x's, because the x in the numerator is only part of a sum.
  • State restrictions from the original denominators, including factors that cancel.
  • Brackets on subtraction. The numerator of the second fraction gets brackets before you distribute the minus.
  • Translations: inside the bracket is opposite and horizontal, outside is matching and vertical.
