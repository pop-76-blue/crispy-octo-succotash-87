Multiplying and Dividing Rational Expressions

MCR3U1 · Unit 2 · Section 2.2

You multiply and divide rational expressions exactly as you do fractions: PQ · RS = PRQS, and dividing means multiplying by the reciprocal.

The method

  1. Factor every numerator and denominator.
  2. Division: flip the second fraction (use its reciprocal) and change ÷ to ×.
  3. Divide out any factor that appears in a numerator and a denominator.
  4. Multiply what is left across.
  5. Restrictions: exclude values that make any denominator 0 in the original problem. For division, also exclude values that make the divisor's numerator 0.
