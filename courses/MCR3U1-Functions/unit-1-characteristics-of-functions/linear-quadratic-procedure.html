Solving Linear-Quadratic Systems

MCR3U1 · Unit 1 · Section 1.7

A linear-quadratic system is one line and one parabola. Solving it means finding where they meet, so you are looking for points of intersection.

The procedure

  1. Make y the subject in both equations.
  2. Set the two expressions for y equal (substitute one into the other) and move everything to one side.
  3. Solve for x (factor, or use the quadratic formula).
  4. Sub each x back into the linear equation to find y. Write each answer as a point (x, y).

How many solutions? Check the discriminant in step 3

b² − 4ac < 0
No real solutions. The line misses the parabola.
b² − 4ac = 0
One solution. The line is a tangent, touching at one point.
b² − 4ac > 0
Two solutions. The line is a secant, cutting through at two points.
