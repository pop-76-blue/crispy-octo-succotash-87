Max and Min Word Problems

MCR3U1 · Unit 1 · Section 1.3

Steps for word problems: (1) define your variables, (2) write an equation, (3) solve it, (4) answer the question in a sentence. For a quadratic model, the vertex gives the maximum or minimum.
Try this: For each problem, slide to your best guess of the maximum before using algebra, then find the exact vertex by completing the square and see how close you were. Also decide which form (standard, factored or vertex) answers each part fastest.
