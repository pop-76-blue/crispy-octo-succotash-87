Line Meets Parabola

MCR3U1 · Unit 1 · Section 1.7

Move the line (y = mx + b) around a parabola y = a(x − h)² + k. The discriminant of the equation you get by setting them equal tells you whether the line misses (negative), is a tangent (zero), or is a secant (positive).
Parabola
Line

How the readout works

Setting a(x − h)² + k = mx + b and moving everything to one side gives a quadratic Ax² + Bx + C = 0, where A = a, B = −(2ah + m) and C = ah² + k − b. The readout reports B² − 4AC. This is exactly step 3 of the procedure on the previous page.

Try this: With a = 1, h = 2, k = −3, m = 1, drag b until the verdict flips from "secant" to "no intersection". Predict the exact b where it switches (it's the tangent value), then press the snap button to see whether you were right. Then change m to 3 and repeat.
