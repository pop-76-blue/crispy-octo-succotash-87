Max and Min by Completing the Square

MCR3U1 · Unit 1 · Section 1.3

The vertex of a parabola is its maximum or minimum point. Vertex form f(x) = a(x − h)² + k shows it directly: the optimal value is k, and it happens at x = h.

The 7 steps

  1. Write the terms in decreasing powers of x.
  2. Group the two x-terms: (ax² + bx) + c.
  3. Factor a out of the grouped terms: a(x² + (b/a)x) + c.
  4. Complete the square inside: add and subtract (half of the x-coefficient)².
  5. Write the perfect square trinomial as a squared binomial.
  6. Expand the square brackets so the subtracted value gets multiplied by a. This is where most errors happen.
  7. Simplify the constants. Then read the vertex. a > 0 gives a minimum, a < 0 gives a maximum.
