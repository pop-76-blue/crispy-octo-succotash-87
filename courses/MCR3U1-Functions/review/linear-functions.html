Linear Functions

MCR3U1 · Prerequisite Review

Slope-intercept form: y = mx + b. m is the slope (rise/run), b is the y-intercept. Two lines are parallel if they have the same slope, and perpendicular if their slopes are negative reciprocals of each other (m₁ × m₂ = −1).

Finding an equation from two points

When you're not given slope-intercept form directly, use rise/run for the slope, then either substitute into y = mx + b or use point-slope form: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁).

Example: Find the equation of the line through (5, 5) and (−10, −4).

1. Slope: m = (5 − (−4)) / (5 − (−10)) = 9/15 = 3/5
2. Substitute a point into y = mx + b: 5 = (3/5)(5) + b → 5 = 3 + b → b = 2
3. Equation: y = (3/5)x + 2
