Exponent Laws Checker

MCR3U1 · Prerequisite Review

Before applying a product or quotient rule, always check the bases are the same. Follow order of operations for everything else.

The laws

Product rule
am × an = am+n
Quotient rule
am ÷ an = am−n
Power of a power
(am)n = amn
Zero exponent
a0 = 1 (a ≠ 0)
Negative exponent
a−n = 1 / an

Work through these

Type your answer as a single power (e.g. 2^2) where the instructions ask for it, or the evaluated number where they ask you to evaluate. Then reveal the steps to check your reasoning either way.
