Factoring Path Finder

MCR3U1 · Prerequisite Review

Before anything else: always check for a GCF first. Every method below assumes you've already pulled out the greatest common factor, if there is one. Skipping this step is the most common factoring mistake.

The decision tree

  • Look for a GCF
  • 2 terms → Difference of squares (a² − b²)
  • 3 terms → Trinomial (simple or complex)
  • 4 terms → Grouping
