3.4 Applying Newton's Second Law

Putting weight, normal force, applied force, and friction together in one problem.

Most real problems have more than one force acting at once. The process is always the same:

  1. Draw the free-body diagram and identify every force.
  2. Find any forces you can calculate directly (e.g. weight: Fg = mg).
  3. On a flat surface with no vertical acceleration, the normal force balances gravity: FN = Fg.
  4. Add up the forces along the direction of motion to get Fnet.
  5. Solve a = Fnet / m.

g = 9.8 N/kg (same number as 9.8 m/s² from Unit 1 — it's both the acceleration due to gravity and the number of newtons of weight per kilogram of mass).
