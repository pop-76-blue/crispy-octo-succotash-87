3.3 Newton's Second Law

Fnet = ma — the relationship between net force, mass, and acceleration.

Newton's Second Law: the acceleration of an object is directly proportional to the net force acting on it, and inversely proportional to its mass.

Fnet = ma

Units: force in newtons (N), mass in kilograms (kg), acceleration in m/s². One newton is defined as the force needed to accelerate a 1 kg mass at 1 m/s² (1 N = 1 kg·m/s²).

Rearranged: a = Fnet / m, or m = Fnet / a.
