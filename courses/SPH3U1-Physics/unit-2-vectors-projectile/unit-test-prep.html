Quick Reference

1D Kinematics

Distance (scalar) vs. displacement (vector). Speed = distance/time, velocity = displacement/time. Slope of a d-t graph = velocity. Slope of a v-t graph = acceleration. Area under a v-t graph = displacement.

Big Five Equations

Five equations linking v1, v2, a, Δd, Δt — each missing exactly one variable. Pick the equation that's missing the variable you don't know and don't need.

Vectors

Add vectors graphically tip-to-tail, or algebraically using components: vx = v cosθ, vy = v sinθ. Recombine with the Pythagorean theorem and inverse tangent.

Projectile Motion

Horizontal velocity is constant. Vertical velocity changes at g = 9.8 m/s². Solve the vertical motion for time first, then use that time in the horizontal motion.

Mixed Practice

Try each on paper first, then reveal the answer.

Free Fall
A ball is thrown straight down from a bridge. It is in the air for 3.2 s and hits the water below with a velocity of 134 m/s [down]. How tall is the bridge?
Taking down as negative: v2 = −134 m/s, Δt = 3.2 s, a = −9.8 m/s².
Use the equation that doesn't need v1: Δd = v2Δt − ½aΔt²
Δd = (−134)(3.2) − ½(−9.8)(3.2)² = −428.8 + 50.2 ≈ −379 m
The bridge is about 380 m tall (rounded to 2 sig figs, matching the precision of the given values). You can check this on the Big Five solver — just note you'll need to type something into v1 to use the tool (it won't affect this particular equation's answer, since this equation doesn't use v1 at all).
Vectors
A hiker walks 30 m [E], then 40 m [N]. Find the hiker's total displacement (magnitude and direction).
This is a right triangle: magnitude = √(30² + 40²) = √2500 = 50 m.
Direction: θ = tan-1(40/30) ≈ 53° north of east.
Displacement ≈ 50 m [E53°N].
Vector Components
Two forces act on an object: 60 N at 20° and 45 N at 100° (both measured from East). Find the resultant force.
Force A: Ax = 60cos(20°) ≈ 56.4 N, Ay = 60sin(20°) ≈ 20.5 N
Force B: Bx = 45cos(100°) ≈ −7.8 N, By = 45sin(100°) ≈ 44.3 N
Resultant components: Rx ≈ 48.6 N, Ry ≈ 64.8 N
Magnitude: R = √(48.6² + 64.8²) ≈ 81.0 N, at θ = tan-1(64.8/48.6) ≈ 53° above East.
Check it on the Vector Components tool.
Projectile Motion
A ball is kicked at 20 m/s at an angle of 30° above the ground. Find its time of flight, range, and maximum height.
vx = 20cos(30°) ≈ 17.3 m/s, vy0 = 20sin(30°) = 10 m/s
Time of flight: Δt = 2vy0/g = 20/9.8 ≈ 2.04 s
Range: R = vx × Δt ≈ 17.3 × 2.04 ≈ 35.3 m
Max height: h = vy0²/(2g) = 100/19.6 ≈ 5.1 m
Check it on the Projectile Motion simulator.
