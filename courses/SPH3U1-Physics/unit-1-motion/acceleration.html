1.4 Acceleration

How velocity changes over time — and what a v-t graph tells you.

Acceleration is the rate at which velocity changes over time:

a = (v2 − v1) / (t2 − t1) = Δv / Δt

Units: typically m/s². If a car's acceleration is 3 m/s², its velocity increases by 3 m/s every second.

On a velocity-time graph, acceleration is the slope. And something extra: the area under a v-t graph gives you displacement — because velocity × time = distance.
