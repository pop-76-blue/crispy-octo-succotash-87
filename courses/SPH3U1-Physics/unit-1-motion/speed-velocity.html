1.2 Speed & Velocity

Speed is a scalar. Velocity is a vector. They're easy to mix up — here's how to tell them apart.

Average speed (vavg) is the total distance travelled, divided by the total time:

vavg = Δdtotal / Δt   (scalar)

Average velocity (vavg) is the displacement, divided by the total time:

vavg = Δd / Δt   (vector)

Units for both: m/s (or km/h). The two are only equal when you move in a straight line without ever reversing direction.

Converting Units: km/h ↔ m/s

Use the factor label method — multiply by a fraction that cancels the units you don't want:

72 km/h × (1000 m / 1 km) × (1 h / 3600 s) = 20 m/s

km/h: 72
m/s: 20.0
