ASCII Table (1–127)

Unit 1  ·  1.2.5 The ASCII Table — full reference, one row per character

Every character has one decimal ASCII value, shown here alongside the same value in three other bases you'll use throughout this unit: binary (base 2), octal (base 8 — see the Octal & Other Number Systems page), and hexadecimal (base 16 — see the Hexadecimal Converter page). Codes 1–31 and 127 aren't printable characters at all; they're control signals left over from early teletype equipment (like line feed or "end of transmission"), shown here in gray.
