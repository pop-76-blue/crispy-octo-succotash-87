TinkerCAD Setup Guide

Unit 3  ·  Preparation — before Lab 1

TinkerCAD is a free, browser-based tool for designing and simulating electronic circuits — no software install needed. Every lab in this unit gets built and saved inside it.

  1. Create an account
    Go to tinkercad.com and choose Sign In, then Create a personal account if you don't already have one.
  2. Sign in
    Once your account exists, sign in at the same address.
  3. Find the Learning Center
    From the main page, choose Resources, then Learning Center, then Learn Circuits.
  4. Do the starter lessons
    Work through as many of the circuit lessons shown as you can — Start Simulating, Editing Components, and Wiring Components cover exactly the skills the labs need.
  5. Know your way home
    Click the TinkerCAD icon in the top-left corner any time to get back to your Home Page and see all your saved designs.
  6. Start a new circuit
    From your Home Page, click + New and choose Circuit to open a blank workspace for a lab.
Try this: Before Lab 1, complete at least the "Start Simulating" and "Wiring Components" lessons in the Learning Center — they walk through exactly the click-drag-and-release wiring motion Lab 1 will ask you to do.
