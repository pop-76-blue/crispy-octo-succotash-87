Breadboard Basics

Unit 3  ·  Breadboards — what's actually connected to what

A breadboard looks like a plain grid of holes, but underneath, metal clips join certain holes together into groups. Nothing is connected to anything else unless you know these rules:

  • The two power rails at the top and bottom (marked + and −) each run the full length of the board as one long connected strip.
  • The main terminal area is split top and bottom by a center gap. Within each half, every hole in the same column (5 holes, rows a–e or f–j) is connected — but nothing crosses the center gap.
