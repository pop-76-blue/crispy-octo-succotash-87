Truth Table Builder

Unit 2  ·  2.1–2.7 — type any logical expression, get its complete truth table

A truth table lists every possible TRUE/FALSE combination of the variables and the result of the expression for each one. The number of rows is always 2n, where n is the number of different variables — a variable that appears twice still counts once.

Order of operations (from your notes, 2.5)

PriorityOperatorExample
1stBrackets ( )NOT (A OR B) — the bracket is worked out first
2ndNOTNOT A AND B means (NOT A) AND B, not NOT (A AND B)
3rdANDP OR Q AND R means P OR (Q AND R)
4thORDone last

Operators at the same level are done left to right. Your notes also use BUT to mean AND, and the C/Java symbols && (AND), || (OR) and ! (NOT) — the builder understands all of these. Your notes treat XOR as a kind of OR but don't rank it in the list above, so the builder lets you choose; when in doubt, use brackets around XOR.

Are two expressions equivalent?

Two expressions are equivalent when they give the same result on every row.

Try this: Before typing an expression in, write its truth table on paper — and write the grouping with brackets first. Then compare with the builder. If a column disagrees, look at the “Grouped as” line: a wrong grouping is the most common mistake. Also try P OR Q AND R and (P OR Q) AND R in the equivalence checker to see exactly which rows differ.
