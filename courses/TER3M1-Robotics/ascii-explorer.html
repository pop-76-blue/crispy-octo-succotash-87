Unit 1  ·  1.2.5 The ASCII Table

ASCII gives every character a unique decimal number, so devices can agree on what a stream of 1s and 0s actually means. The character is never sent as decimal, though — it's sent as an 8-bit binary pattern, one bit at a time.
