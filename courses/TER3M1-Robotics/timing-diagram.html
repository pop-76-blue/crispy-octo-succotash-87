Timing Diagrams

Unit 1  ·  1.2.6 Timing Diagrams

A timing diagram shows what a single wire in a circuit is doing over time — high (current flowing, a 1) or low (no current, a 0). Each interval lasts the same fixed amount of time. Reading the diagram left to right gives you the bit pattern being transmitted.
